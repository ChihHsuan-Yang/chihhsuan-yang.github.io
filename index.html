Chih-Hsuan (Bella) Yang

Collaborative AI, grounded in science.

I am Chih-Hsuan Yang, also known as Bella Yang, a Postdoctoral Researcher at Argonne National Laboratory. I study how AI agents reason together, use feedback, and learn from experience.

My earlier work focused on AI for materials and molecular discovery, scientific machine learning, and BioTrove. Research at Google X and Corteva connected these interests to agentic systems and preference-based post-training.
