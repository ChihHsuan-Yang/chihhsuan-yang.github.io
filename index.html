Chih-Hsuan (Bella) Yang

LLM agents: collaboration, memory, and learning.

I am Chih-Hsuan Yang, also known as Bella Yang, a Postdoctoral Researcher at Argonne National Laboratory. I build research harnesses for LLM agents and multi-agent systems. My agentic evaluation work compares collaboration protocols, traces how solvers use reviewer critiques, and measures memory utility and inference quality-cost trade-offs.

At Corteva, I trained preference models from experimental outcomes and adapted language models with DPO and PPO, incorporating scientist preferences when the model was uncertain. At Google X, I studied information flow, coordination, and bidding among stakeholder agents. These experiences connect my agent research to preference-based post-training and agentic AI for Science, building on earlier work in materials and molecular discovery and BioTrove.

My first-author work includes Precise but Uncoupled (NeurIPS 2026 Main Track) and Cost-Aware Protocol Routing (Findings of EMNLP 2026). I am also a first author of BioTrove (NeurIPS 2024 Datasets and Benchmarks Spotlight).

Accepted · NeurIPS 2026 main track

Precise but Uncoupled

Traces corrective feedback through reviewer detection, the next answer response, and verified repair, showing why accurate review alone need not improve final outcomes.

Published · Findings of EMNLP 2026

Cost-Aware Protocol Routing

Evaluates supervised protocol routing, held-out generalization, and confidence calibration across reasoning tasks. Separates predicting failure from predicting which collaboration protocol is worth its additional inference cost.

Wrong but Useful

Uses paired message-ablation replay to measure whether an intermediate message helps or harms downstream team reasoning, distinguishing message correctness from its contribution to the final answer.
