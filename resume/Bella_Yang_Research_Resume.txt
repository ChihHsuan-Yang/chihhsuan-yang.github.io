Selected Accepted Publications

Precise but Uncoupled: Reviewer Precision Does Not Guarantee Critique Uptake in Multi-Agent Math Reasoning (https://chihhsuan-yang.github.io/NeurIPS26_Precise-but-Uncoupled/)
First author. NeurIPS 2026 main track, accepted.
Separates reviewer error detection from critique uptake and verified repair, showing why precise review alone does not ensure better final answers.

LLMs Can Predict Failure Risk, But Struggle to Predict Which Collaboration Protocol Pays Off: Cost-Aware Protocol Routing Across Reasoning Tasks (https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.1275/)
First author. Findings of EMNLP 2026.
Audits the gap between predicting failure and choosing a cost-effective collaboration protocol, using matched four-protocol evaluations and two solver families.

BioTrove: A Large Curated Image Dataset Enabling AI for Biodiversity (https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/b92854f80ba4feefb973959b259dbc2c-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.html)
Co-first author. NeurIPS 2024 Datasets and Benchmarks, Spotlight.
Research-grade biodiversity data, vision-language models, and zero-shot evaluation across species, life stages, and taxonomic levels.

MolGen-Transformer: A Molecule Language Model for the Generation and Latent Space Exploration of Organic Molecules (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.commatsci.2026.114549)
First author. Computational Materials Science 269, 114549, 2026.
Transformer-based molecular generation and controlled latent-space exploration for molecular and materials discovery.
