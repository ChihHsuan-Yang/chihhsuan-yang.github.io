Research Contributions and Accepted Publications

Precise but Uncoupled: Reviewer Precision Does Not Guarantee Critique Uptake in Multi-Agent Math Reasoning (https://chihhsuan-yang.github.io/NeurIPS26_Precise-but-Uncoupled/)
First author. NeurIPS Main Track, accepted. | 2026
Designed controlled analyses separating reviewer error detection, a solver's response to critique, and verified answer repair. Identified why accurate review alone need not improve final outcomes, informing agent feedback design.
Paper and project (https://chihhsuan-yang.github.io/NeurIPS26_Precise-but-Uncoupled/)  |  Code (https://github.com/ChihHsuan-Yang/NeurIPS26_Precise-but-Uncoupled)  |  Data (https://huggingface.co/datasets/AgentsSci/NeurIPS26_Precise-but-Uncoupled)

LLMs Can Predict Failure Risk, But Struggle to Predict Which Collaboration Protocol Pays Off: Cost-Aware Protocol Routing Across Reasoning Tasks (https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.1275/)
First author. Findings of EMNLP, published. | 2026
Compared four reasoning protocols on 4,181 math problems, with checks across scientific reasoning tasks and two solver families. Evaluated supervised routing, held-out generalization, confidence calibration, and inference quality-cost trade-offs; distinguished failure prediction from protocol-specific value.
Paper (https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.1275/)  |  Code (https://github.com/ChihHsuan-Yang/EMNLP_Cost-Aware-Protocol-Routing)  |  Data and router (https://chihhsuan-yang.github.io/EMNLP_Cost-Aware-Protocol-Routing/)

BioTrove: A Large Curated Image Dataset Enabling AI for Biodiversity (https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/b92854f80ba4feefb973959b259dbc2c-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.html)
